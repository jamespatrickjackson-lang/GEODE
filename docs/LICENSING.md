# GEODE licensing and third-party content

**Software licence:** Apache License, Version 2.0 (Apache-2.0), published in the repository root as [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE).

## What Apache-2.0 covers

Unless an individual file states otherwise, the Apache-2.0 licence applies to the GEODE/GeoSee project-authored software and related development material **to the extent its contributors or rightsholders have the authority to license that material**. This includes project-authored application source, code, scripts, configuration, tests and accompanying software documentation. Preserve existing copyright and attribution notices. The [`NOTICE`](../NOTICE) file records project attribution.

The licence permits use, modification, redistribution and commercial integration, subject to its conditions. The project remains a research and software-development alpha; this licence is not a claim of scientific accuracy or fitness for a regulated purpose.

## What the software licence does not automatically cover

A public repository is not evidence that every file was authored by GEODE or can be relicensed. In particular, **third-party scientific datasets, geological and survey data, maps, aerial/satellite/terrain imagery, photographs, externally sourced figures, papers, referenced reports, trademarks and separately licensed dependencies** retain their own rights and conditions. GEODE does not grant additional rights over them.

These materials may appear in `projects/`, `data/`, `docs/assets/`, generated visualization assets, demonstration packs and historical technical records. **A directory name alone is not a licensing determination.** GEODE-created derivatives of third-party data may still carry source-specific conditions. For redistribution outside this repository, check applicable source terms and attribution first.

Third-party software dependencies remain under their respective licences. The GEODE licence does not override them.

## Patent scope

Apache-2.0 includes a contributor patent grant in Section 3, limited by the terms of that section. Choosing Apache-2.0 for GEODE does **not** independently license unrelated inventions or an entire separate patent portfolio. Nothing in this note adds restrictions to, or enlarges, the Apache-2.0 licence.

## Questions about a particular asset

If a file's provenance or applicable permission is unclear, treat its reuse terms as **unverified** rather than assuming the Apache software licence covers it. Review the linked project manifests and original source terms before redistributing that asset.
