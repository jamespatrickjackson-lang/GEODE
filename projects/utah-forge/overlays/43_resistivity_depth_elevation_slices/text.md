**Resistivity Depth Slices**

These horizontal planes are resistivity slices through the Phase 3 MT inversion model at fixed elevations: +1500 m, 0 m, and −2500 m above sea level.

Each slice shows the lateral distribution of resistivity at that depth horizon. The logarithmic colour scale is consistent with the full-volume view.

Depth slices allow pattern comparison across horizons: conductive zones that persist with depth, resistive bodies that terminate, and lateral transitions that may correspond to lithological or structural boundaries.

The MT station network is shown for spatial reference — the inversion has measured support where stations are present.
