**PSDM + MT + 58-32 Top-of-Granite Reference**

This page keeps the existing PSDM surrogate, MT context, and 58-32 source-location view, then adds the top-of-granite survey-pick reference from the addendum.

The yellow line is a source-derived interpreted reference, not a recovered complete horizon volume. The 58-32 calibration pick is shown at 1200 m below the 1800 m datum near XL-105 / IL-93.

Survey-pick context: roughly 850-1000 m below datum to the east, deepening toward about 1500 m near the valley axis, with north trend around 1600 m and south around 1400 m. Pick uncertainty is +/-50 m.

Intra-granite energy is treated as multiples / side reflections and is not rendered as granite structure.
