Vertical panels cut through the resistivity model along any chosen line — not limited to the grid axes. Each panel shows log₁₀(ρ) along its trace from surface to depth, and several can be stood together as a fence to read structure across the volume. Co-registered with terrain, footprint, and wells.

In conventional reports a section is fixed at acquisition or processing time — the reader gets the lines someone else chose, on separate flat figures, and cannot re-cut the model to follow a feature.

Our approach The section line is chosen in the scene. A panel is cut along any azimuth — down a well, across a conductive zone, between two points of interest — and shown in true 3D position alongside the others.

The difference The viewer interrogates the model on their own terms rather than reading fixed figures. A fence of panels reveals how structure changes across the site, all in one spatial frame.

Data quality Source: GDR 1255 (FORGE Phase 3 MT, 122 stations). Product: 3D resistivity inversion volume, vertical sections along arbitrary lines. Scalar: log₁₀(ρ). CRS: NAD83 UTM Zone 12N (EPSG:26912).
