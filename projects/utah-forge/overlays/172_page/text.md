Resistivity Volume
The full MT resistivity inversion rendered as a continuous 3D volume — every model cell shaded and accumulated so the structure reads as a solid body of varying log₁₀(ρ) rather than discrete points or shells. Conductive and resistive regions blend through the volume, co-registered with terrain, footprint, and wells.

In conventional reports the full model is never shown as a whole — it is sampled into plan slices and sections, and the reader integrates those samples into an imagined volume.

Our approach The entire inversion is rendered at once. Transparency and colour let the viewer see internal structure — where conductive zones sit relative to resistive bedrock — through the body, navigable from any angle.

The difference The model is presented as the continuous field it actually is. Internal relationships that slices can only hint at become directly visible, with no manual reassembly of separate panels.

Data quality Source: GDR 1255 (FORGE Phase 3 MT, 122 stations). Product: 3D resistivity inversion volume. Scalar: log₁₀(ρ). CRS: NAD83 UTM Zone 12N (EPSG:26912).
