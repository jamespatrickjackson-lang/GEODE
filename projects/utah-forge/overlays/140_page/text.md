The 3D MT resistivity inversion volume rendered as coloured points — one point per model cell centre, each shaded by log₁₀(ρ). The full structure floats in the same coordinate frame as the terrain and footprint, so resistive and conductive zones read directly in space.

In conventional reports resistivity is shown as a stack of separate plan views and cross-sections — a series of flat slices the reader assembles into a mental 3D model.

Our approach The entire inversion volume in one view. The point cloud reveals the overall shape and distribution of the resistivity structure at once, navigable from any angle, before any single slice is isolated.

The difference The viewer sees the whole model as a spatial object rather than inferring it from 2D panels. Where the conductive and resistive bodies sit relative to terrain, footprint, and each other is immediately apparent.
