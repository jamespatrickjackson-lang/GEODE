MT station network.

The yellow markers are measured magnetotelluric acquisition stations parsed from source EDI headers.

Status: measured.

Provenance: 122 MT sites acquired for the Utah FORGE Phase 3 MT survey and carried in the repo as source-derived scene-local markers from `FORGE_MT_190812_EDIs.zip`.

The markers are symbolic for readability. Coordinates remain data-derived from the existing station artifact.

This page does not mark station interpretation as validated and does not alter the source MT files.
