Utah FORGE Stage 2 Phase 3 MT resistivity.

Three primary data layers are active by default.

The yellow markers are measured magnetotelluric acquisition stations parsed from source EDI headers.

The full-density point cloud is a source-derived visualization of every positive-resistivity cell center from the updated MT inversion model. It is colored by log10 resistivity in Ohm-m, with no crop of the source model frame and no vertical exaggeration.

The top-surface aids are separate: the existing translucent preview-derived ground cap remains available, and the added full-density top-contact surface is derived by retaining the highest MT cell center per occupied XY grid cell. Neither is public topo, lidar, DEM terrain, measured ground, or interpreted geology.

The authored slice layer remains available for comparison and presentation experiments. The 90,000-point preview remains available as a QA layer, hidden by default.

This Stage 2 pass is renderer and data-density work, not final story-page presentation work. Future data families remain inactive until explicitly scoped.
