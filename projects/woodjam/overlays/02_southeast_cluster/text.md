The property straddles a contact between Late Triassic Nicola Group volcanic rocks and the Takomkane Batholith, the intrusive body that drove mineralization through the district. Younger Kamloops Group volcanic cover obscures portions of the western property, while older Slide Mountain Terrane rocks frame its eastern edge. The known copper deposits — Deerhorn, Megabuck, Takom, Three Firs, Southeast — cluster along the intrusive contact, each one a distinct mineralization centre tied to the same regional system. The geology pattern is consistent with porphyry deposits emplaced into a permissive volcanic host, with cover sequences masking what may sit beneath.
